# Policy Record

This document tracks the formal legislative engagement behind the AI Corridor of the Americas. It is kept factual and current on purpose — this work gets cited in public talks and press, and precision here protects the credibility of everything else in this repository.

## H.Res.649 — 118th Congress (2023–2024)

- **Title:** Calling on the United States to champion a regional artificial intelligence strategy in the Americas to foster inclusive artificial intelligence systems that combat biases within marginalized groups and promote social justice, economic well-being, and democratic values.
- **Sponsor:** Rep. Adriano Espaillat (D-NY-13)
- **Introduced:** August 8, 2023
- **Referred to:** House Committee on Foreign Affairs, and the Committee on Science, Space, and Technology
- **Status:** Did not receive a floor vote before the 118th Congress ended
- **Source:** [congress.gov/bill/118th-congress/house-resolution/649](https://www.congress.gov/bill/118th-congress/house-resolution/649)

## H.Res.836 — 119th Congress (2025–2026)

- **Title:** Identical to H.Res.649 above.
- **Sponsor:** Rep. Adriano Espaillat (D-NY-13)
- **Introduced:** October 28, 2025 (reintroduction of H.Res.649)
- **Referred to:** House Committee on Foreign Affairs, and the Committee on Science, Space, and Technology
- **Status:** Introduced; in committee as of this writing
- **Source:** [congress.gov/bill/119th-congress/house-resolution/836](https://www.congress.gov/bill/119th-congress/house-resolution/836)

## What this is, precisely

A House resolution of this kind expresses the "sense of the House." It is not a bill that becomes law if passed — it's a formal statement of position. That's worth being exact about in any keynote, press interview, or investor conversation: this is real, sustained congressional engagement across two Congresses, and it is advocacy, not enacted policy. Overstating it in either direction — calling it "passed legislation" or dismissing it as "just a website claim" — would both be wrong. State it as what it is.
