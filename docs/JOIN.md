# How to Join

This project is built to let someone anywhere in the hemisphere — or outside it — find a real way in, without needing an introduction or a warm contact. Pick the path that matches you.

## Developers and researchers

No application, no gatekeeping. Go to [multimodal-ai-americas](https://github.com/GENIA-Americas/multimodal-ai-americas), read its README, and open an issue or a pull request. That's the open research repository; it's the lowest-friction way in.

If your interest is platform engineering specifically, look at [Glapagos-Backend](https://github.com/GENIA-Americas/Glapagos-Backend) (Python, MIT licensed) and check its open issues for a starting point.

## Educators

If you're building AI-literacy curriculum anywhere in the Americas and want to compare notes or contribute case material, email [admin@genia.ai](mailto:admin@genia.ai) with subject line "Education." This connects back to the K-12 education work referenced in [README.md](../README.md).

## Policymakers and government staff

Start with [POLICY.md](POLICY.md) for the exact legislative record. If you want to discuss the regional AI strategy conversation for your own jurisdiction, email [admin@genia.ai](mailto:admin@genia.ai) with subject line "Policy."

## Investors and institutional partners

Start with [DUE_DILIGENCE.md](DUE_DILIGENCE.md) — it's built specifically so you can verify the claims independently before a first conversation. Then reach out to [admin@genia.ai](mailto:admin@genia.ai) with subject line "Partnership."

## Everyone else

Read [MANIFESTO.md](MANIFESTO.md) — it's short — and if it resonates, the fastest next step for almost anyone is starring or watching the [GENIA Americas GitHub organization](https://github.com/GENIA-Americas) so you see the work as it happens, rather than waiting for a press cycle to tell you about it later.
