Every claim below links to a primary source. If a link ever breaks or a fact goes stale, open an issue — see [CONTRIBUTING.md](../CONTRIBUTING.md).

## What we have not independently published proof of, and say so

- Exact network reach ("12+ regions, 35 nations") is stated on [racefor.ai](https://www.racefor.ai) but we have not published the underlying methodology or region list in this repository. If you need that for a funding or partnership decision, request it directly rather than citing the headline figure.
- Named partner relationships (SingularityNET, Seguritech, Intermestic) are shown as logos on the public site. A logo indicates a relationship exists or existed; for current status and scope, confirm with the partner or with us directly.

## How to verify us the fast way

1. Read [POLICY.md](POLICY.md) and cross-check both bill numbers on Congress.gov yourself.
2. Browse the commit history on any of the three linked repositories.
3. Email [admin@genia.ai](mailto:admin@genia.ai) with specific due-diligence questions — corporate structure, funding history, specific partnership scope. We would rather answer directly than have you guess from a website.
